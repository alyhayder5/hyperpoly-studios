Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. If you email us, we use your message only to reply. Each of our products has its own privacy policy.

This policy covers hyperpolystudios.com, run by Hyperpoly Studios (“we”, “us”). Our products, such as site.txt and AquaDock, have their own privacy policies, linked below.

What we collect on this website

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-up, no contact forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it does not store anything in your browser.

Services this website relies on

  • Hosting. Like every website, the server that delivers these pages may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details when you visit. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Search Console. We use it to see how the site appears in Google Search. It reports search data that Google already has and does not add tracking to these pages.

When you email us

If you write to hyperpolystudios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to work with you on your enquiry or project. We don’t sell it, share it for marketing or add you to a mailing list. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

Our products

  • site.txt privacy policy: the Chrome extension that turns websites into text.
  • AquaDock privacy policy: the Chrome extension that turns your New Tab into a Mac-inspired desktop.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any emails we hold from you, or ask what we hold, by writing to the address above.

Children

This website is not directed at children and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We’ll update this page and the date above if anything changes.
